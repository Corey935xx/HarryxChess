THE BASICS

How to play chess

The aim is to checkmate your opponent's king — put it under attack so it has no legal escape.

♙

Take your turnWhite moves first. Select one of your pieces, then choose a highlighted square.

♘

Learn the piecesPawns move forward, rooks move in straight lines, bishops diagonally, knights in an L-shape, queens in any straight line and kings one square.

♔

Protect your kingYou cannot make a move that leaves your own king in check. Checkmate ends the game.

½

Draws can happenA game can also end in a draw, including stalemate or repeated positions.
